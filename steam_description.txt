[h1]Long Tables[/h1]

Tables of the same material built next to each other are drawn as one table: the top runs through from tile to tile and one leg stands under every join. Build a banquet table as long as your hall, or as wide: rows, blocks, L, T, U, plus and ring shapes all join up.

It is purely cosmetic. The tables stay separate tables to the game, nothing is stored in your save, and removing the mod puts every table back as it was.

The chairs in the pictures are from New and Improved Chairs, a separate mod. Long Tables changes tables only.

[h2]What joins[/h2]
[list]
[*]Finished tables on the same level, next to each other west to east or north to south, made of exactly the same material. Quality and decorations make no difference.
[*]Tables that touch only at a corner do not join.
[*]Wood, stone, metal and glass each keep their own surface, in the material's own colour.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[b]Requires DFHack (free on Steam), kept up to date. Without it this mod does nothing.[/b] Tested on Dwarf Fortress 53.17 (Steam Windows build, run under Wine on macOS) with DFHack 53.17-r1. Native Windows, Linux and Proton are untested.

[h2]Installing[/h2]
[list]
[*]Subscribe and wait for Steam to finish downloading.
[*]Fully exit the game, then start it through DFHack and load your fortress.
[/list]
Existing saves work. Do not add the mod to a world's mod list at world generation; it adds nothing to a world.

[h2]What the mod puts on your computer[/h2]
Nothing but the Workshop download and the copy the game itself makes of every subscribed mod. It is a plain DFHack script of two files (the script and the table art), with no compiled plugin. It writes no files of its own, does not use the network and stores nothing in your save.

To switch it off without removing it, run this in the DFHack console:
[code]overlay disable long-tables.map[/code]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fortress mode with the graphical tiles only.
[*]Long tables are drawn from the game's standard table; a mod that changes the table sprite is not picked up.
[/list]
